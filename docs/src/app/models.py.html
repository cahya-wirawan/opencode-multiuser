    app/models.py

    83 lines, 6 of 6 definitions explained, 8 line notes. View original

    In the guide: Architecture overview, Authentication and roles, Slots and workspace leases

    SQLAlchemy ORM models for the portal's database: users, the pool of workspace slots, and workspaces. It also defines the status and role enums. The partial unique indexes declared here encode two invariants: one account per OIDC identity, and one active workspace per user and project.
